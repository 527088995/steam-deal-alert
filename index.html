FAQ

What is Steam Deal Alert?

Steam Deal Alert is an Android app that tracks Steam game prices, daily deals, and wishlist items. You can see price history and get notified when games you care about go on sale.

How does Steam Deal Alert work?

The app uses public Steam and store data to show current prices, historical discounts, and sale info. You can browse deals, add games to track, and enable notifications for price drops.

Can I track my Steam wishlist?

Yes. You can connect or mirror your wishlist and the app will track those games and alert you when they go on sale or hit a discount you care about.

Is the app free?

Core features are free. Optional Pro features may offer more tracking and alerts. Check the Google Play listing for current details.

Does the app show Steam price history?

Yes. You can view historical prices and past discount levels for games so you can judge whether the current sale is a good deal.

Why use this instead of checking Steam manually?

Checking dozens of wishlist games or hunting for daily deals on Steam takes time. The app aggregates deals and tracks your list in one place and sends alerts so you don’t have to check constantly.
